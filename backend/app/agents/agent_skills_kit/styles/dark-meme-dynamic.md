## When to use

Use this skill when creating short, high-energy videos (15-60 seconds) that don't show a person's face but rely heavily on meme culture, rapid visual changes, and sound effects to maintain engagement. Perfect for meme compilations, quick commentary, reaction content (without face), and viral-style short-form content.

## Core Principles

### 1. Extreme Pacing
- **Ultra-quick cuts**: Average shot length 0.3-0.8 seconds. Never exceed 1.5 seconds on any shot.
- **Rapid-fire transitions**: Use a different transition almost every cut (whip pan, glitch, zoom, spin, shake). Transitions should be 0.1-0.2 seconds.
- **Beat-synced editing**: Every cut, transition, and effect must land on the music beat. Use high-BPM tracks (160-200 BPM).
- **No static moments**: The screen should always be moving, changing, or animating. If nothing happens for 2 seconds, you've lost the viewer.

### 2. Meme-Centric Visuals
- **Meme integration**: Include popular meme images, GIFs, and video clips that appear and disappear quickly (0.5-1 second each).
- **Reaction visuals**: Use reaction faces, exaggerated expressions, and cartoon elements that pop in sync with audio cues.
- **Text as punchlines**: Short, impactful text that appears with animation (pop, glitch, typewriter) to deliver jokes or commentary.
- **Visual callbacks**: Reference earlier memes or jokes later in the video for continuity and inside humor.

### 3. Sound Design Dominance
- **Music is foundation**: Use trending meme music, phonk, or hyper-pop with clear, punchy beats.
- **Sound effects as punctuation**: Whoosh, boom, vine boom, record scratch, airhorn, glass break, etc. Every transition should have an SFX.
- **Audio cues for visuals**: Memes and text should appear exactly on sound effect hits or vocal cues.
- **Layered audio**: Combine music, SFX, and voiceover (if any) without clashing. Use ducking when voice speaks.

### 4. Faceless Dynamic Composition
- **Screen space utilization**: Fill the entire frame. Use zooms, pans, and position changes to create movement even within a single clip.
- **Layered elements**: Stack multiple visual elements (video + meme overlay + text + graphic) with careful z-index ordering.
- **Camera shake & impact**: Add subtle screen shake on punchlines or big moments.
- **Color pop**: Use high saturation and contrast. Occasionally desaturate then saturate for emphasis.

### 5. Structure for Short-Form
- **Hook in 0-2 seconds**: First frame must be a meme, shocking image, or text that grabs attention.
- **Pattern and variation**: Establish a rhythm (e.g., meme → text → reaction → meme) then break it occasionally to surprise.
- **Build to climax**: Stack increasingly intense or funny memes toward the middle/end.
- **End on high note**: Final meme or text should be the strongest, leaving viewers wanting to replay.

## Technical Guidelines

### Format & Specs
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical) for TikTok/Reels/Shorts. 1080x1080 square also acceptable.
- **Frame rate**: 60fps for smooth slow-motion capability. Export at 30fps if file size is concern.
- **Duration**: 15-60 seconds optimal. Never exceed 90 seconds for this style.
- **Bitrate**: 8-15 Mbps for quality.

### Audio Sync
- **Beat mapping**: Identify all downbeats in music track before editing. Mark them on timeline.
- **SFX library**: Have a collection of meme sound effects ready. Organize by category (whoosh, impact, comedic, etc.).
- **Voiceover**: If used, must be fast-paced, energetic, and heavily compressed (punchy). Use text-to-speech or heavily processed voice for robotic meme effect.

### Visual Effects Library
- **Transitions**: Whip pan, glitch transition, zoom blur, spin, shake, flash, cut (hard cuts are also valid if on beat).
- **Overlays**: Film burn, light leak, VHS static, scan lines, chromatic aberration (sparingly).
- **Text animations**: Pop-in, typewriter, glitch text, bounce, spin-in.
- **Motion graphics**: Animated emojis, bouncing stickers, floating elements.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Slow pacing**: Any shot over 1.5 seconds breaks the energy. Cut faster.
- **Repetitive transitions**: Using the same transition every time becomes boring. Vary them.
- **Off-beat editing**: If cuts aren't on the beat, it feels sloppy. Lock to music.
- **Crowded screen**: Too many overlapping elements create visual chaos. Maintain hierarchy.
- **Copyright issues**: Using copyrighted meme content may get claims. Use royalty-free or transformative use.
- **Weak hook**: First 2 seconds are critical. Start with your strongest meme/text.
- **Inconsistent energy**: Don't have a slow middle section. Maintain high tempo throughout.
- **Poor audio mix**: SFX overpowering music or voice. Balance levels properly.

## Advanced Techniques

### 1. Layered Timing
- **Triple rhythm**: Combine (a) music beat for cuts, (b) sound effect for meme appearances, (c) vocal delivery for text. All three should align or create intentional polyrhythm.
- **Pre-beat anticipation**: Show a visual 0.1 seconds BEFORE a sound effect hits for comedic timing.
- **Post-beat linger**: Let a meme stay 0.2-0.3 seconds AFTER its audio cue for emphasis.

### 2. Meme Stacking
- **Nested memes**: Show a meme within a meme (e.g., reaction video playing on phone screen within main video).
- **Meme evolution**: Start with a classic meme, then show a modern remix of it later.
- **Unexpected combinations**: Pair unrelated memes for surreal humor.

### 3. Visual Punctuation
- **Impact frames**: Freeze frame for 0.2 seconds on the funniest moment, then resume.
- **Zoom spikes**: Rapid zoom-in (0.3s) on a reaction face or text punchline.
- **Color flash**: Screen flash (white or colored) on big moments.
- **Text size extremes**: Huge text for punchlines, tiny text for subtle jokes.

### 4. Audio Manipulation
- **Pitch shift**: Raise or lower voice/meme audio for comedic effect.
- **Reverse audio**: Play a sound backwards before a reveal.
- **Layered SFX**: Combine 2-3 sound effects on a single moment for extra impact.
- **Music stutter**: Briefly pause music on a beat for dramatic effect.

## Workflow Recommendations

1. **Music first**: Select your meme track. Mark all beats on timeline.
2. **Meme assembly**: Gather all meme images/videos/clips you plan to use. Organize by category.
3. **Rough cut**: Place memes on timeline synced to beats. Don't worry about length yet.
4. **Add SFX**: Layer sound effects on every transition and key moment.
5. **Text overlay**: Add animated text that appears with audio cues.
6. **Refine timing**: Trim each clip to exact duration. Ensure perfect beat sync.
7. **Effects & overlays**: Add transitions, screen shake, color effects.
8. **Audio mix**: Balance music, SFX, and voice. Use compression on voice.
9. **Color grade**: Apply high-contrast, saturated look. Use LUTs for consistency.
10. **Final review**: Watch multiple times. Cut anything that doesn't land. Export.
